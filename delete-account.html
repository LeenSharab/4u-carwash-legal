Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your 4U Carwash account and associated personal data directly from inside the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the 4U Carwash app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Go to the Profile or Account page.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Review the warning and confirm the deletion.

Data that will be deleted

  • Your user account and login access.
  • Your profile information, including your name, phone number, and optional email address.
  • Saved account preferences and profile-related information.
  • Other personal data linked to your account, unless retention is required by law.

Data that may be retained

Certain transaction, payment, booking, fraud-prevention, tax, legal, or dispute-related records may be retained for the period required by applicable laws and legitimate business obligations. Retained records will not be used to keep your account active.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. After your account is deleted, you may not be able to recover your profile, booking history, rewards, or account access.
